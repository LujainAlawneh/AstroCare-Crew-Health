Added only: Entertainment tab, Sudoku, local chess, and book reading/downloads. Existing files other than index.html are unchanged. All prior script blocks and original medical/report/exercise/journal functions are retained unchanged.

Sudoku has 9x9 puzzles with a unique-solution check, three difficulty targets, undo, hints and local progress. Chess uses bundled chess.js 1.4.0 for legal move validation, supports two local players or a beginner offline opponent, undo, board flip and local PGN progress.

Two full Project Gutenberg editions are embedded with their original credits/license: Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland (Lewis Carroll, ebook 11) and Around the World in Eighty Days (Jules Verne, ebook 103). Books can be read and downloaded as UTF-8 text without a connection. Personal .txt books up to 5 MB can be imported and kept locally using IndexedDB. Reading positions are saved in localStorage. Browser data can be cleared; keep original downloads.

No game, reading or entertainment data is sent to medical APIs or used in clinical decisions. No external package installation or CDN is needed to run the application. Start as before: python server.py, then http://127.0.0.1:8765.
